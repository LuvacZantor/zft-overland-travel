Overland Travel Procedure

This system resolves travel through a hex by determining terrain difficulty, assigning roles, resolving travel checks, tracking party momentum, and generating an event that is influenced by both the environment and the party’s performance.


Procedure Checklist

Follow these steps for each hex:

  1. Roll Event (establish base event)

  2. Establish the HEX State

  3. Choose Roles

  4. Determine Role DC

  5. Each Player Rolls

  6. Determine Individual Outcomes

  7. Tally Party Results

  8. Determine Travel Quality

  9. Check Party Momentum

  10. Shift Party Momentum

  11. Resolve the Event

Roll Event

Roll 1d20 to determine the event type. (this may change from this random starting point)

Choose Roles

Each player selects one role:

  • Guide

  • Scout

  • Lookout

  • Forager

  • Pathfinder

  • Quartermaster

Role Limits

  • Each role can be filled by only one character

  • Roles do not stack

  • Unfilled roles provide no benefit

Support

  • One additional character may support a role

  • The supporter must have a relevant proficiency

  • The role check is made with advantage

Determine Role DC

Determine the DC for the Hex State that will be used for roll checks.

Role DC = Terrain Base DC + Region Danger Modifier

Each Player Rolls

Each player rolls an ability check appropriate to their chosen role.

  • If a role has a supporter, the roll is made with advantage

Check Party Momentum

Momentum represents the party’s current expedition condition. At the start of Overland travel, will usually be Neutral, unless otherwise modified by the DM.

State

Description

Deteriorating

👉 Momentum carries over between hexes and shifts during travel.

Shift Party Momentum

Move Momentum one step:

  • Successful Travel → one step toward Favored

  • Poor Travel → one step toward Deteriorating

  • Neutral Travel → no change

Momentum may shift only one step per hex.

Event Types

Encounter

A standard interaction. This may be combat, social, or neutral depending on the situation.

Complication
An obstacle or problem that creates difficulty, pressure, or delay. Could lead to a threat.

Discovery (link)
A beneficial or informative finding such as a location, resource, or opportunity.

Quiet Travel (link)
No significant event occurs. The party travels without interruption.
